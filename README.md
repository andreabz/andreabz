# Andrea Bazzano

**Analytical chemist · Environmental chemistry · Data analysis · Statistical modelling**

I am a chemist working at the intersection of **analytical chemistry, environmental measurements and quantitative data analysis**.

My work focuses on turning experimental data into reproducible analyses, with particular attention to **measurement uncertainty, method validation, calibration, experimental design and statistical modelling**.

I use **R and Quarto** to make analyses transparent, reproducible and easier to review.

## What I work on

* Analytical and environmental chemistry
* Method validation and performance evaluation
* Calibration and analytical measurement
* Experimental design
* ANOVA, linear models and mixed-effects models
* Reproducible data analysis and reporting
* R, data.table and Quarto
* Quality-oriented approaches to laboratory work

## Selected projects

### Analytical chemistry and environmental research

[**psa_pb_ny-alesund**](https://github.com/andreabz/psa_pb_ny-alesund)  
Reproducible analysis supporting research on potential source areas of atmospheric lead reaching Ny-Ålesund.

### Method validation

[**r-report_validazione**](https://github.com/andreabz/r-report_validazione)  
A reproducible framework for organising requirements, experimental data, calculations and reporting in analytical method validation under an ISO/IEC 17025-oriented workflow.

### Calibration design

[**calibration_design**](https://github.com/andreabz/calibration_design)  
Simulation-based exploration of calibration design, heteroscedasticity, weighting, non-linearity and concentration prediction in chemical analysis.

### Statistical modelling in analytical chemistry

[**analytical-anova**](https://github.com/andreabz/analytical-anova)  
A progressive exploration of ANOVA, linear models, ANCOVA, interactions, diagnostics, robustness and mixed models using analytical-chemistry examples.

[**mixed_models**](https://github.com/andreabz/mixed_models)  
An experimental perspective on mixed-effects models, focusing on experimental units, non-independence, variance structure and uncertainty.

### Analytical software

[**SIprecisa**](https://github.com/ARPAL-liguria-it/SIprecisa)  
R/Shiny application for evaluating precision and trueness of analytical measurements, with automated testing and deployment through Docker/ShinyProxy.

## How I approach data analysis

I am interested less in applying statistical techniques mechanically and more in understanding **how the experimental design, measurement process and scientific question determine the appropriate analysis**.

The same principle guides my work on reproducibility: calculations, assumptions, data transformations and reporting should be explicit and, where possible, executable again.
